Overview

Atopic Dermatitis Skin Model

Labskin-AD is a full-thickness human skin model engineered to mimic key characteristics of atopic dermatitis.

The model reproduces impaired skin barrier function and inflammatory responses associated with atopic dermatitis, providing a controlled platform for disease-focused research.

Its disease-relevant phenotype enables researchers to investigate skin barrier dysfunction, inflammatory signalling and the biological mechanisms involved in atopic dermatitis.

Labskin-AD can also be used to evaluate therapeutic interventions, barrier-repair technologies and sensitive-skin formulations within a reproducible human tissue environment.
