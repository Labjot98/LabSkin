Overview

Full-Thickness Pigmented Human Skin Model

Labskin-M incorporates functional melanocytes within the epidermal compartment to replicate human skin pigmentation processes.

The model enables researchers to investigate melanogenesis and pigment regulation within a physiologically relevant human tissue environment.

Its full-thickness architecture provides a realistic setting for studying pigment production, distribution and skin tone modulation.

Labskin-M supports pigmentation research across cosmetic, pharmaceutical and fundamental skin biology applications.
