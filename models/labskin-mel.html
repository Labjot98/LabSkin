Overview

3D Human Melanoma Skin Model

Labskin-Mel combines full-thickness skin architecture with melanoma cells to recreate key aspects of the human tumour environment.

The model provides a physiologically relevant platform for studying melanoma biology, tumour progression and interactions between melanoma cells and surrounding skin tissue.

By incorporating melanoma cells within a structured human skin environment, Labskin-Mel enables researchers to investigate tumour behaviour beyond the limitations of conventional monolayer cultures.

The model supports oncology research, therapeutic screening and molecular analysis across academic and drug discovery applications.
