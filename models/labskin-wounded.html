Wounded Labskin-S provides a controlled human-relevant platform for investigating wound closure, tissue regeneration and the response of wounded skin to therapeutic interventions.

Overview

Human-Relevant Wound Healing Research

Wounded Labskin-S provides a controlled human tissue environment for investigating wound healing and tissue repair.

The model enables longitudinal monitoring of wound progression and supports quantitative assessment of healing endpoints.

Controlled wound creation allows treatments, dressings and regenerative therapies to be evaluated under reproducible experimental conditions.

Wounded Labskin-S supports applications including wound closure, re-epithelialisation, biofilm research, burn treatment and regenerative medicine.
